## Purpose

Open Inbit Snipe-IT, log in, and recognize the dashboard. Browser/no-phone handling is help, not a required workflow step.

## Current Steps

1. `Open Snipe-IT` - tap the recognizable shortcut on the phone interface.
2. `Log in` - enter account details and tap `Inloggen`.
3. `Controleer dashboard` - confirm the dashboard is visible.

## Help

- Geen account: vraag beheerder of supervisor.
- Wachtwoord kwijt: vraag je supervisor om het wachtwoord te resetten.
- Geen telefoon: open a browser at `https://snipe.inbit/`.
- Sessie verlopen: log opnieuw in.
