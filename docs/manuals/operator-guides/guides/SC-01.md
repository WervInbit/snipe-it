## Purpose

Find an asset by QR or manual search, open the result, and verify that the digital record matches the physical device before any work starts.

## Steps

1. `Open de scanner` - choose either the dashboard `Scan QR` card or the camera icon in the top bar.
2. `Kies scannen OF handmatig zoeken` - either allow camera access and hold the
   physical QR label steadily in view, or enter the exact asset tag under
   Handmatige invoer and choose Zoeken. The exact unique match opens the asset.
   Camera failure uses the other route. Multiple results: compare serial and
   model before opening the matching asset row. No result: recheck the tag;
   ask for help if uncertain. Unresolved identity follows step 3's halt condition.
3. `Controleer hetzelfde apparaat` - after either route, compare the model/type
   in the title and Assettag/Serienummer on Info with the physical device.

the record; ask the supervisor to verify it. Continue once the identity matches.
