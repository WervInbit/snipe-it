## Purpose

Find an asset by QR or manual search, open the result, and verify that the digital record matches the physical device before any work starts.

## Steps

1. `Open de scanner` - choose either the dashboard `Scan QR` card or the camera icon in the top bar.
2. `Scan het label` - hold the physical QR label steadily in the mobile camera view.
3. `Zoek handmatig` - when scanning is unavailable, search using the asset tag, QR value, or a verified supported serial number and choose the result.
4. `Controleer de asset` - compare the asset title, asset tag/model, and status with the physical device.
