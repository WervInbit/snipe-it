## Purpose

Work through the active workflow item by item, record truthful results, add required notes or image evidence, and recognize the saved completion state.

## Steps

1. `Valideer de actieve workflow` - confirm the asset in the breadcrumb before using a result card.
2. `Lees de instructie` - open and read the full instruction before touching a result control.
3. `Voer uit en kies resultaat` - perform the check and choose the truthful `Geslaagd` or `Mislukt` result.
4. `Voeg een notitie toe` - record a concise failure, exception, or relevant measurement.
5. `Voeg foto of schermafbeelding toe` - use `Foto` and `Foto toevoegen` when the check requires image evidence.
6. `Herhaal en controleer afronding` - complete every required card and verify the saved counts.

## Stop

Do not mark a result without performing the check. Stop and ask when the instruction, expected result, or physical device is unclear.

## Evidence Note

The live in-app `Foto` panel and `Foto toevoegen` action are sufficient for the V1 internal review candidate. The device-native camera/file picker is not shown because it varies by device and is outside this guide's required evidence scope.

Version 10 uses current blank-run captures so `Geslaagd` and `Mislukt` remain neutral before step 3. Instruction, note, and photo views keep the complete card visible. Step 1 uses `Valideer de actieve workflow` and shows a complete, readable breadcrumb context. In 4A, the application's yellow outline identifies `Notitie` as active while the red target identifies the note-entry field; this matches 5A, where the active `Foto` section and `Foto toevoegen` action have separate visual roles. Other action targets use measured source-pixel control bounds, and non-critical stop text is left to the help section instead of repeated beside the steps. The front-page `Klaar als` handoff says `Ga verder op de volgende pagina.`

## Complete When

Every required workflow item has a truthful saved result, required notes or image evidence are attached, and the saved run counts are visible.
