## Reader Outcome

The correct user receives the saved temporary reset privately and proceeds to choose a private password.

## Action And Choice Structure

- Identify the same user and open Gebruiker aanpassen.
- Genereer once, preserve login/rights, Save and read success.
- Hand over personally; the user follows AC-02 and never supplies the definitive password as proof.

## Operating Contract And Boundaries

- Generate fills both fields and displays the temporary value below the first field. Do not capture or retain that value.
- A reset does not authorize enabling an inactive account or changing its access.
- Do not send the temporary or private secret in chat, email, notes, tickets or screenshots.
- An uncertain identity pauses the change until clarified. Arrange the private handoff before giving the value to anyone.

## Revision Reasoning

Chosen improvement: Give the whole reset control group a wider lower position so Generate remains legible.

Preserve: Three actions, saved verification and the personal AC-02 handoff.

Placement and tradeoff: No redundant screenshot of the user's later password task; that separate job remains in AC-02.
