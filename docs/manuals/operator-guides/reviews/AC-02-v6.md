## Reader And Reasoning

Intended role: Iedereen. Starting context: Current or temporary local password.
Outcome: The signed-in user has saved a private password and recognizes the success message.

Required alignment: Align the compact account menu, actual repeated field label, visible rule and real saved result.

Chosen reader improvement: Separate the native Save control from the actual success proof, keeping the whole three-field group recognizable.

Preserved support: Three actions, private-password outcome, complete form support and the reset handoff.

Placement/preservation tradeoff: The new mobile crop changes the frame shape; field text recognition, rather than original outer screenshot width, determines usable scale.

## Actions And Choices

- Open the account menu and Wachtwoord wijzigen.
- Enter current, new and confirmation values in their actual field order.
- Save, read the result and use the new password.

## Concrete Changes

- Actueel Nederlands accountmenu en het volledige formulier met drie velden.
- De twee gelijke labels Nieuw wachtwoord zijn op positie uitgelegd.
- Opslaan en de werkelijk opgeslagen succesmelding staan apart; geen kaders om het complete formulier.

## Evidence, Checks And Limits

The mounted application is the separate refurbisher-followups checkout,
not the root checkout. Source fingerprints, actual actors, Dutch locale,
owned synthetic fixtures and save/trial boundaries are recorded in the batch
provenance. Genuine English strings and narrow native status-choice text
remain product gaps. Header roles preserve the intended task allocation;
local seeded/custom grants can be wider than the operational-role document.
Physical printing/component work, destructive catalogue branches and an
unfamiliar-reader/A4 paper trial are unperformed. Artifact checks and editorial
read-back do not establish reader performance or exact acceptance.

The [batch review](remaining-guides-2026-10-20.md) records shared reconciliation,
actual UI trials, unperformed routes and final preservation/check results.
