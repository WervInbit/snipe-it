## Reasoning, Changes And Tradeoffs

Use the [per-guide brief](operational-guides-2026-10-20.md) and current
shared method. Preserves the accepted two-sided six-step teaching structure, full instructions, truthful results, note/photo guidance, privacy and save recovery. The two label modes are explicitly alternative card examples. Real current Dutch cards replace historical English/old controls. Three result/save/photo recovery messages move to their point of use, and only the final side declares completion. Klaar return does not erase failure or release an asset.

## Review And Limits

All seven final pages were rendered at 144 dpi and visually inspected, with
complete relevant baseline pages read for comparison. Normal and alternative
routes, recovery, help placement, completion and actual target recognition
were checked. Generator checks enforce A4 bounds, shared reference geometry,
source/crop/focus bounds and screenshots remaining within their task regions.
These checks establish artifact properties and editorial judgments; they are
not novice-reader trials or exact acceptance.

The [provenance](../../../../resources/manuals/operator-guides/review-rounds/2026-10-20-operational-guides/evidence-provenance.json)
records Dutch settings, actual mounted application and source fingerprints,
owned fixture, real start/note/photo/Done/handoff actions, prepared synthetic
results, native note-field resize and capture navigation recovery. Workflow
progression/component translations and mobile history width remain gaps.
Physical work, print/QA-location validation, negative/override/error trials
and unfamiliar-reader testing are unperformed. No accepted/runtime selection
or historical artifact changed.
