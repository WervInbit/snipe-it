All seven final pages were rendered at 144 dpi and visually inspected, with
complete relevant baseline pages read for comparison. Normal and alternative
routes, recovery, help placement, completion and actual target recognition
were checked. Generator checks enforce A4 bounds, shared reference geometry,
source/crop/focus bounds and screenshots remaining within their task regions.
These checks establish artifact properties and editorial judgments; they are
not novice-reader trials or exact acceptance.

results, native note-field resize and capture navigation recovery. Workflow
progression/component translations and mobile history width remain gaps.
Physical work, print/QA-location validation, negative/override/error trials
and unfamiliar-reader testing are unperformed. No accepted/runtime selection
or historical artifact changed.
