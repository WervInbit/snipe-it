## Changes And Tradeoffs

Impact: guide/evidence/application alignment, with WF-01 v14 as the revised
entry route. Preserves two sides, six steps, instruction/note/photo support,
both help sections, completion and named handoffs. Six visual groups now
contain eight raster placements instead of six.

- Replaces the constructed historical breadcrumb with a real matching asset
  breadcrumb. It identifies the asset; profile/run choice belongs to WF-01.
- Shows both Pass/Fail and Done/Not Done cards, and distinguishes required
  positive results for progression from merely answering every required item.
- Requires the note timestamp to update after typing; explicitly explains
  that an old timestamp on an empty field is not proof of a new save.
- Shows the complete real photo panel and privacy notice. Requires checking
  the returned thumbnail rather than treating file selection as successful upload.
- Replaces the historical run-count screenshot in step 6 with disabled and
  enabled Done states, waits for saves, and returns to the same asset's
  Workflows tab. Failed work remains failed; Done does not release an asset.
- Printed version badge and footer explicitly say Niet geaccepteerd.

Benefit: clearer result labels, saving checks, and completion destination.
Tradeoff: controls in this development account are English; Dutch prose gives
their meanings. The two Done crops deliberately show different existing runs
on the same asset, not a before/after test in which results were changed.
Note/photo frames retain complete cards with slightly narrower evidence
columns to fit the established two-sided layout. This draft's body is 2.45 mm
instead of the earlier 2.55 mm; compare that local readability tradeoff on
physical A4. Other guides' typography is unchanged.
