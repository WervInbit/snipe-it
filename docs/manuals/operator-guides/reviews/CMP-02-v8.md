## Reader And Reasoning

Intended role: Senior Refurbisher. Starting context: Verified asset and new physical component.
Outcome: One new correctly identified component record is created and installed on the intended asset.

Required alignment: Current form controls, definition handoff and actual tracked/baseline behavior.

Chosen reader improvement: Give both complete alternative forms and the resulting identity enough space to read.

Preserved support: The four-action outcome, definition/custom choice, physical comparison and verified tracked result.

Placement/preservation tradeoff: Two continuous sides replace a crowded single side; there is no completion before the necessary result check and no appended new Step 1.

## Actions And Choices

- 1: Open the new component form; an existing record hands off to CMP-01.
- 2: Choose definition A OR agreed one-off custom B; resolve a missing reusable type through Supervisor/CAT-04 before Create.
- 3-4 on side two: compare/place the physical part, Create And Install once, then verify identity, link, Extra and specification.

## Concrete Changes

- Definitie OF afgesproken eenmalige custom-route is expliciet; bestaande registratie gaat naar CMP-01.
- Twee doorlopende zijden houden beide formulieren en de uitkomst leesbaar.
- De ontbrekende definitie wordt voor Create opgelost; Extra en specificatie worden voor afronding gecontroleerd.
- Bij vervanging wordt voor Install/Create gecontroleerd dat het oude onderdeel al is losgekoppeld; onduidelijkheid gaat naar Supervisor.

## Evidence, Checks And Limits

The mounted application is the separate refurbisher-followups checkout,
not the root checkout. Source fingerprints, actual actors, Dutch locale,
owned synthetic fixtures and save/trial boundaries are recorded in the batch
provenance. Genuine English strings and narrow native status-choice text
remain product gaps. Header roles preserve the intended task allocation;
local seeded/custom grants can be wider than the operational-role document.
Physical printing/component work, destructive catalogue branches and an
unfamiliar-reader/A4 paper trial are unperformed. Artifact checks and editorial
read-back do not establish reader performance or exact acceptance.

The [batch review](remaining-guides-2026-10-20.md) records shared reconciliation,
actual UI trials, unperformed routes and final preservation/check results.
