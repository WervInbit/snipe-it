## Cause And General Correction

The earlier rule promoted the owner's request for help finding a target into
an automatic annotation trigger: several visible items. The test generator
then marked nearly every named value, including close crops whose complete
content was the task's check. Measurement and unclipped strokes passed, while
the necessity of each mark was not established. That review cannot support
the earlier claim that all the marks improved the guides.

Review the screenshot and instruction without focus marks first. Preserve
screen orientation and readable target scale. Ask what still needs searching
for or could be confused with a competing item. Add the minimum annotation
that resolves that difficulty; compare with the unmarked crop at the same A4
size. A jointly checked group is a legitimate target as a whole. When the crop
already isolates it, leave the group and its members unmarked. Small targets
among similar icons, candidates in a result list and physical label locations
can still need focus. This replaces a visible-item trigger with a reader need;
it does not establish a numeric maximum.

Exact group names describe access consistently. Effective rights and capture
roles are useful authoring evidence. Add an explanation of a permission in the
guide only where it changes an action or decision; do not turn that evidence
into a new role name. Existing roles, ownership and application permissions
are unchanged by this editorial correction.
