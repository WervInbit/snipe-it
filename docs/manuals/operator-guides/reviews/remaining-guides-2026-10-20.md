## Shared Reconciliation

Apply the current authoring method, task-dependent layouts, exact access-group
names, Dutch application/account locale, full guide references, useful actual
A4 target scale, conditional recoverable help and genuine identity halts.
Review each crop without annotations. A complete form/result normally needs
no outline. Small navigation actions or physical labels can need one.

Retire fixed password recipes, invented role labels, routine STOP banners,
decorative digital QR placeholders, numbered alternatives read as a sequence,
post-completion alignment appendices and DOM-invented success/conflict evidence.
Reuse the useful accepted composition and explanations rather than an arbitrary
new template. CAT-00 is reference; CAT-05 is an indexed collection of independent
maintenance routes. Other continuations keep numbering and completion at the end.

Desired targets: complete Dutch translation, intuitive catalogue learning order,
clear field-to-result relationship and unambiguous readable evidence. Missing
translations and source-retention controls are product gaps. Do not invent a
future Testing completed status, source field or browser-inaccessible setting.
