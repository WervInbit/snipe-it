# Guide Authoring Reasoning Repair

Owner's requested outcome: repair the reasoning used to generate, fix and
adjust guides so it applies generally across the set. The
[authoring method](../authoring-method.md) now governs that process through
AGENTS.md, system.md and maintenance.md. This work changes authoring and review,
without generating guide PDFs or making acceptance decisions.

The owner also clarified the revision objective: accepted guides already
establish a good direction, can improve further, and need substantial updates
for changed application behavior. The method therefore supports both required
system alignment and chosen reader improvements. It assesses the complete
revised guide against the accepted direction, rather than counting corrected
facts or preserving every old design choice. Improved clarity, recognition or
ease of use can justify a change even without a known reader error.

## Causal Repair

The October batch effectively followed this order:

```text
Application changes -> text/image patches -> generic extra page -> fit checks
```

That process selected content through novelty, let existing/template numbering
stand in for task relationships, and fitted available screenshots to frames.
The review then checked the intended interpretation more readily than the
one suggested by the visible page. Existing preservation rules could therefore
be nominally followed while their useful effects were lost.

The repaired process is:

```text
Reader outcome and knowledge
    -> current requirements, desired targets and retired approaches
    -> accepted direction, required updates and intended reader gains
    -> verified facts and relevant reader decisions
    -> ownership, action/choice structure and completion
    -> evidence with a named recognition job
    -> composition and explicit gains/costs
    -> read-back of visible meaning and complete-guide comparison
    -> separately reported integrity, judgment, trials and acceptance
```

Information earns its place by an effect on the reader's outcome. Layout follows
the information's relationships. Evidence is selected to support those
relationships. Existing good properties establish a direction to develop;
improvements retain or improve their useful effects. Required new behavior
must be taught accurately, but does not make an unrelated layout change or
lost recognition inevitable. When the combined guide regresses, revisit how
the new material and improvements were integrated.

This is a change in decision order and evaluation criteria. Adding more
technical pass/fail checks alone would leave the content-selection and
interpretation errors intact. A short concrete reasoning brief now precedes
substantive authoring; local cosmetic work reuses the established outline.

## Standards Reconciliation

The method referenced shared contracts, but its brief and maintenance sequence
did not explicitly reconcile a baseline with later standards and owner
decisions. That allowed a good older artifact to be treated as authority for
an inherited point already abandoned. The method now requires this comparison
before baseline reuse and again across the complete rendered revision.

Current requirements, desired targets, retired/rejected approaches and open
decisions are distinct inputs. Their source and scope are recorded in the
brief. Current requirements join the application-update plan; desired targets
guide improvements without inventing unavailable product behavior. Reused
text and new supplementary pages receive the same review. Acceptance preserves
the historical artifact, while later decisions govern the next revision.

The standards map links the existing language, identity, reading structure,
evidence, warning, composition and operational-scope contracts. It does not
create a new universal template. An existing components.md statement limiting
guides to two pages was also scoped to floor tasks, matching the declared
administration/reference exceptions in system.md, layouts.md and the CAT plan.

## Reasoning Exercises And Countercases

These are editorial desk exercises using known feedback and contrasting
situations. Their results demonstrate how the method distinguishes cases;
they do not claim novice/operator trials or improved new-PDF performance.

| Situation | Reasoning under the method | Countercase that limits the principle |
| --- | --- | --- |
| An accepted guide works, but its choice could be easier to compare. | Keep the established direction and test a clearer grouping against the complete baseline. Reduced hesitation can justify improvement without a known failure. | A more symmetrical composition that shrinks useful evidence or separates a condition from its choice loses reader support; acceptance is neither a freeze nor permission for arbitrary redesign. |
| An older USR-04 combines disabling, deleting, restoring and check-in work. | Reconcile it with the later disable-login-only specification and owner correction. Carry forward useful teaching support, while removing the superseded scope from the next version. | CAT-05 has an explicit independent-maintenance purpose; several clearly selected routes can be appropriate there. This is a scoped decision, not a ban on every multi-route guide. |
| The owner prefers Testing completed without a separate Awaiting approval state. | Record the desired status direction and unresolved operational/product work. Teach the verified current controls until the chosen transition is implemented and evidenced. | An implemented and verified status change does require new wording/evidence. A desired target is neither permission to invent a control nor a reason to preserve obsolete instructions after that change. |
| AC-01: a recent-device list was added to the dashboard. | Login success is the reader outcome. The list does not change the login action or success check, so it does not earn an instruction or a smaller dashboard crop. | SC-01 can use a recent link to reopen the device being handled, with the same physical-identity check. |
| SC-01: scan and manual search occupy adjacent numbered cards. | These are routes to the same result. Their grouping and wording must communicate one choice followed by convergence. Fix the relationship even when inherited from an older version. | WF-02 reading an instruction and recording its result are sequential actions; combining them as alternatives would be incorrect. |
| An additional page starts again at step 1. | First determine whether it continues the same outcome or starts an independent task. Numbering must communicate that relationship. | CAT-05's named, independently selected maintenance routes can each start at 1. |
| A new crop contains extra screen context and makes the button smaller. | Identify which control/context the reader needs, compare its actual final recognition size, and adjust the crop or composition. Matching the old frame size is insufficient. | An isolated enlarged button can be harder to find if its surrounding panel/navigation disappears. |
| AST-05: release now requires confirmation. | The required confirmation changes the task, so integrate it after status selection and before the saved end state. Replace the obsolete automatic-save claim and use a coherent result. | A second page is legitimate if this necessary decision and evidence cannot remain readable. Page count is not itself the defect. |
| A release capture shows missing-work override instead of clean release. | The ordinary release task requires evidence for that ordinary state. Keep the override example within an explicit authorized exception; do not let capture availability determine the main route. | HELP or a guide specifically about resolving a blocked release should start from the issue state. |
| CAT-00: sum and distinct numeric results need explanation. | The reader is learning a relationship. Use a concept comparison at the relevant specification/result explanation, with examples and guide ownership. | CAT-03 actually configuring the rule needs an operational choice before Save; identical facts have a different instructional role. |
| USR-02/05: status access is separate from general permissions. | Explain the relationship where effective access is decided/tested. Name the status-control handoff and return; do not automatically append a second completed permission procedure. | A dedicated status-access task can warrant its own clearly scoped route if the owner needs to perform it independently. |
| Global language is Dutch but captures are English. | Check the state the visual claims, including account preference and visible labels. Locale configuration is part of evidence preparation. | Missing application translations cannot be solved by claiming that a Dutch setting makes the visible control Dutch or by replacing its pixels. |
| A figure badge or focus rectangle is misaligned. | Reuse the valid task outline. Repair and inspect the local target geometry without opening a new guide-scope/layout change. | A wrong callout caused by an obsolete UI state may require new evidence and a corresponding instruction correction. |
| A new printer-management panel exists. | In a floor labeling guide, teach selecting the relevant available printer and reading its result where needed. Queue administration belongs to its administrator outcome. | A guide requested for managing enabled printers should model that administrator's task directly. |
| A completely new guide has no accepted predecessor. | Establish its reader question, knowledge, actions/choices or reference structure first; use the closest useful recipe and evidence jobs. | Lack of a baseline is not permission for a fixed step count or invented UI. Real operational uncertainty still needs a source/decision. |

The crucial generalization is the cause and its limit. "Never restart at 1",
"always make screenshots larger", "never add a page" and "preserve every old
layout" would each fail a legitimate case in this table.

## Coverage Across All Twenty Five Subjects

The method is applied by reading relationship, not by the subject's family
color. This coverage map checks that every current subject has a relevant
reasoning path; it does not fix its future page count or step count.

| Reading structure | Current subjects | What the reasoning must establish |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Short entry | AC-01 | Starting point, login action and recognizable successful entry. |
| Compact task, with choices where real | AC-02, SC-01, AST-04, WF-01, CMP-01, CMP-02, CMP-04, USR-01, USR-03, USR-04 | Actual action order, choice conditions, identity, relevant recovery and observable outcome. |
| Procedure needing more explanation | AST-03, AST-05, WF-02, USR-02, USR-05, CAT-01, CAT-02, CAT-03, CAT-04 | Coherent start-to-result flow, sufficient field/choice explanations, conditional routes, save/confirm evidence and page handoffs. |
| Route overview | AST-02 | The current situation, appropriate next guide and return/next destination without duplicated execution. |
| Problem lookup | HELP-01 | A recognizable symptom, the applicable recovery and a reachable destination. No implied procedure across unrelated problems. |
| Conceptual reference | CAT-00 | The reader's question, meaningful relationships/examples and where to apply them. |
| Independently selected maintenance tasks | CAT-05 | Route selection, each route's entry/impact/result and clear local numbering. |
| Evidence verification | CAT-06 | What a source supports, what remains unresolved and where the verified fact returns. |

## Changes To The Working Sources

- AGENTS.md requires the method for guide work at session initialization.
- system.md connects the method to production, precedence and review claims.
- maintenance.md puts the brief and structure decisions before generator edits.
- layouts.md derives recipe changes from reader relationships and recognition.
- screenshots.md requires planned evidence jobs, actual locale and target scale.
- Guide specifications identify frozen HTML as reproduction evidence, rather
  than authority over scope, the specification or owner decisions.
- README, HANDOFF, decisions and the review index identify this general method
  as the current work direction. The earlier single-pilot advice remains history.

Historical output/generators retain their bytes for reproduction. New revisions
must follow the method even when reusing their rendering components. No automatic
quality score or field-presence test is represented as proof of reader usability.
