## Reader And Reasoning

Intended role: Supervisor. Starting context: Physical device, manufacturer label and label printer.
Outcome: One physical device has one matching registration and an applied label opening that same asset.

Required alignment: Integrate reserved identifiers, current entry/model selection, required status note and managed printer/size controls before their actions.

Chosen reader improvement: Separate saved tag/serial, model title, print controls and physical orientation into recognition jobs.

Preserved support: The accepted two-sided, eight-step registration-to-verified-label flow and full physical placement photo.

Placement/preservation tradeoff: No third-page registration restart. Use focused result details while retaining the physical photo and the final identity check.

## Actions And Choices

- 1-4: search first; open Nieuwe Asset; retain reserved tag, copy physical serial, choose exact manufacturer model code, status/note and Save once.
- 5-6: compare saved identity, open the asset, choose label template, loaded size and printer before one print.
- 7-8: place the physical label with orientation support, scan and compare the same identity.

## Concrete Changes

- Nieuwe Asset, voorgestelde tag, S/N, exacte modelcode en Statusnotitie sluiten aan op de huidige toepassing.
- Eerst Asset openen voor printer en formaat; de snelle printknop gebruikt standaardinstellingen.
- Acht doorlopende stappen, echte opgeslagen identiteit en de bestaande plaatsingsfoto.

## Evidence, Checks And Limits

The mounted application is the separate refurbisher-followups checkout,
not the root checkout. Source fingerprints, actual actors, Dutch locale,
owned synthetic fixtures and save/trial boundaries are recorded in the batch
provenance. Genuine English strings and narrow native status-choice text
remain product gaps. Header roles preserve the intended task allocation;
local seeded/custom grants can be wider than the operational-role document.
Physical printing/component work, destructive catalogue branches and an
unfamiliar-reader/A4 paper trial are unperformed. Artifact checks and editorial
read-back do not establish reader performance or exact acceptance.

The [batch review](remaining-guides-2026-10-20.md) records shared reconciliation,
actual UI trials, unperformed routes and final preservation/check results.
