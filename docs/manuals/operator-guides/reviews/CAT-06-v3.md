## Reader And Reasoning

Intended role: Supervisor. Starting context: One uncertain identity/fact, physical label and manufacturer source.
Outcome: The one confirmed identity/fact returns to its owning entry task, or the exact unresolved question is handed over without invented data.

Required alignment: Reverify official scope and retain uncertainty without extrapolating an exact configuration.

Chosen reader improvement: Carry numbering to page two and make the three result routes alternatives with proportionate amber unresolved guidance.

Preserved support: Three-page worked example, readable physical/source evidence, supported/unsupported fact distinction and return handoffs.

Placement/preservation tradeoff: No new input procedure or assumed product feature is added merely because a source is available.

## Actions And Choices

- 1: read the physical manufacturer label and state one concrete question.
- 2: compare official source scope, distinguish family fact from exact SKU or optional configuration evidence.
- 3: A confirmed model identity -> CAT-01; B confirmed specification fact -> CAT-02; C unresolved -> preserve the question/leave optional data empty or defer required-identity creation.

## Evidence, Checks And Limits

The mounted application is the separate refurbisher-followups checkout,
not the root checkout. Source fingerprints, actual actors, Dutch locale,
owned synthetic fixtures and save/trial boundaries are recorded in the batch
provenance. Genuine English strings and narrow native status-choice text
remain product gaps. Header roles preserve the intended task allocation;
local seeded/custom grants can be wider than the operational-role document.
Physical printing/component work, destructive catalogue branches and an
unfamiliar-reader/A4 paper trial are unperformed. Artifact checks and editorial
read-back do not establish reader performance or exact acceptance.
