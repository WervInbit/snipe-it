## Reader And Reasoning

Intended role: Senior Refurbisher. Starting context: Verified asset, same component and own tray.
Outcome: The same tracked identity is in the operator's tray and has no current asset link.

Required alignment: Current full action/dialog and the genuine saved unlinked-tray result.

Chosen reader improvement: Use separate close result details instead of shrinking an entire component-detail page.

Preserved support: Four actions, locked-serial explanation, physical move and final link/status check.

Placement/preservation tradeoff: The complete result check replaces a whole-page miniature; it does not broaden the guide into inventory lifecycle work.

## Actions And Choices

- Identify the component and choose Naar tray.
- Recheck the actual dialog and locked serial.
- Remove/place the physical part and confirm once.
- Verify In Tray, Asset: N.v.t. and unchanged identity.

## Concrete Changes

- Volledige Naar tray-actie en echt bevestigingsvenster met vergrendeld serienummer.
- In Tray en Asset: N.v.t. zijn als leesbare details getoond.
- Tag en serienummer blijven behouden; geen algemene STOP-balk.

## Evidence, Checks And Limits

The mounted application is the separate refurbisher-followups checkout,
not the root checkout. Source fingerprints, actual actors, Dutch locale,
owned synthetic fixtures and save/trial boundaries are recorded in the batch
provenance. Genuine English strings and narrow native status-choice text
remain product gaps. Header roles preserve the intended task allocation;
local seeded/custom grants can be wider than the operational-role document.
Physical printing/component work, destructive catalogue branches and an
unfamiliar-reader/A4 paper trial are unperformed. Artifact checks and editorial
read-back do not establish reader performance or exact acceptance.

The [batch review](remaining-guides-2026-10-20.md) records shared reconciliation,
actual UI trials, unperformed routes and final preservation/check results.
