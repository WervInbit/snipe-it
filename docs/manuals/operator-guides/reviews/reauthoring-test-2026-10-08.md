## Rendered Read-Back And Baseline Tradeoffs

AC keeps three familiar columns. New login evidence is 50 mm wide rather than
36 mm; the dashboard is 50 mm wide rather than 48 mm, with its useful tile
about 22 mm wide. Its frame is shorter because unrelated status/recent content
is excluded; the controls have not been shrunk to make the frame fit.
Captions and help are larger, with clearer account fields and completion.

SC carries forward asymmetric entry, camera photo and manual support. The
compact manual crop retains Handmatige invoer, field and Zoeken; it removes
the camera-error banner and unrelated identifier hint while keeping the scanner
location established by step 1. A shared Dutch asset page and its linked Info
detail replace the old detached result/identity examples. Help occurs at its
condition rather than repeating the identity STOP below the page.

AST's vertical steps spend two sides on one task instead of appending a second
procedure after an earlier completion. Current required workflows replace an
old historical-pass-only impression. The physical-label photo remains readable
as a location aid; actual component content now explains assumptions. Its
status-note and confirmation targets are larger, while verification uses the
saved badge and selected history tab. The normal example is clean, rather than
a missing-work or cancelled dialog standing in for release.

Initial rendered issues were corrected before retention: a caption too close
to the SC image border, image labels near control/caption text, and AST step 6
body near its step badge. The final four pages were inspected at 144 dpi, with
physical target dimensions and complete source-crop/mark boundaries reviewed.
Their expected reader benefit remains an editorial judgment.
